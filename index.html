FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?
No. The visual builder compiles your flow to clean TypeScript, but you can ship without writing a line. Developers can open the generated code anytime.
Which AI models can my bot use?
Any model with an API — GPT, Claude, open-source, or your own fine-tune. Wire it once and switch per flow or per user.
How does billing work?
You pay a flat plan price that includes a monthly pool of AI generations. Extra generations and messages above your plan are metered with tabular precision — see the limits table above.
Can I self-host or deploy to my own region?
Pro runs on our autoscaling edge runtime across 12 regions. Enterprise adds VPC deploy and dedicated capacity with a 99.99% SLA.
Is there a free tier?
Yes — Free includes 25 AI generations and 5,000 messages a month, forever. No card required to start.
